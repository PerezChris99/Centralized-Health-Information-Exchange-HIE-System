Healthcare Challenges in Uganda

Uganda's healthcare system serves over 45 million people across diverse regions. The fragmented nature of health records creates significant barriers to quality care.

Key Challenges
  • Fragmented Records
    Patient data scattered across paper files and isolated digital systems
  • Limited Interoperability
    Different hospitals using incompatible record systems
  • Duplicate Testing
    Patients undergoing repeated tests due to unavailable prior results
  • Referral Gaps
    Critical information lost during patient transfers between facilities
