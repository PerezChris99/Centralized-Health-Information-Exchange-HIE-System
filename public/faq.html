Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Health Information Exchange System

General Questions

The Health Information Exchange System is a centralized platform that enables healthcare providers across Uganda to securely share and access patient health information. It connects hospitals, clinics, and healthcare facilities to ensure continuity of care regardless of where a patient seeks treatment.

The system is designed for healthcare providers including doctors, nurses, clinical officers, and administrative staff at registered healthcare facilities. Patients can also access their own health records through the patient portal.

Yes, the system is designed to serve all 134 districts of Uganda. We are progressively onboarding healthcare facilities across the country, with priority given to regional referral hospitals and high-volume facilities.

Security & Privacy

We implement multiple layers of security including end-to-end encryption, secure authentication with JWT tokens, role-based access control, and comprehensive audit logging. All data transmissions are encrypted using TLS 1.3, and data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.

Only authorized healthcare providers with valid credentials can access patient records. Access is controlled through role-based permissions, and every access is logged for audit purposes. Patients have the right to know who has accessed their records.

Yes, the system is designed to comply with Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, as well as international healthcare data standards including HL7 FHIR. We regularly undergo security audits to ensure compliance.

Using the System

Click the "Sign Up" button and select "Healthcare Provider" as your account type. You'll need to provide your professional details including your medical license number. After registration, your credentials will be verified before you gain full access to the system.

Yes, the system is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and computers. You can access the platform through any modern web browser on your mobile device.

You can store comprehensive patient information including demographics, contact details, physical characteristics (height, weight, BMI), medical history, allergies (food and medication), diagnoses, treatments, prescriptions, lab results, and patient photos.

Currently, the system requires an internet connection to function. However, we are developing offline capabilities that will allow healthcare providers in areas with limited connectivity to work offline and sync when connection is restored.

Technical Support

On the login page, enter your email address and click "Forgot password?" A password reset link will be sent to your registered email address. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

You can report technical issues through our Contact page or email us directly at support@hie-uganda.org. Please include as much detail as possible about the issue, including any error messages you encountered.

Yes, we provide training resources including user guides, video tutorials, and live training sessions for healthcare facilities onboarding to the system. Contact us to schedule a training session for your facility.
